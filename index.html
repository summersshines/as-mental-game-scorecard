Scoring guide

1. Plan, max 3

Info gathering (1): Assess wind, lie, hazard zones and the situation.

Decision (2): A clear choice based on present capabilities.

2. Commit, max 4

See visuals (1): A crystal clear flight image.

Feel shot (1): Immersion in tempo and contact.

Full commitment (2): 100% conviction over the ball.

3. Execute and release, max 3

Free execution (1): Swing freely at the target.

Post-shot release (2): Gain confidence, or learn and leave it in the past.

Maximum score per shot is 10 points. Tap a score to set it, tap it again to clear it. When all three pillars are scored the shot folds up and the next shot opens. Tap a folded shot to reopen it.

Focus on process over outcome
